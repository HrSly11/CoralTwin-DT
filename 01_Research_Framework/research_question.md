# Research Question: CoralTwin-DT

## Central Research Question

> **¿Qué estrategias de conservación y restauración de corales maximizan la resiliencia del ecosistema arrecifal ante olas de calor marinas y acidificación oceánica?**
>
> *(Which coral conservation and restoration strategies maximize reef ecosystem resilience under compounded marine heatwaves and ocean acidification?)*

---

## Deconstructed Specific Research Questions (SRQs)

### SRQ 1: Biophysical Dynamics & Compounded Stress
*How do accumulated degree heating weeks ($DHW$) interact non-linearly with decreasing carbonate saturation ($\Omega_{\text{arag}}$) and photosynthetically active radiation ($PAR$) to trigger sudden catastrophic coral bleaching and structural mortality?*

### SRQ 2: Cyber-Physical Predictive Modeling
*Can an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees (XGBoost) and deep multi-layer neural networks, coupled with physical oceanographic reanalysis, accurately predict localized bleaching risk categories (`Low`, `Medium`, `High`) 4 to 12 weeks in advance with a Macro-F1 score exceeding $0.90$?*

### SRQ 3: Non-Linear Threshold Discovery & Explainability
*What are the explicit ecological tipping points identified by TreeSHAP game-theoretic feature attribution across environmental covariates (e.g., critical DHW threshold under acidified vs ambient pH conditions)?*

### SRQ 4: Forward Scenario Evaluation & Interventions
*Under projected CMIP6 climate pathways (SSP2-4.5 vs SSP5-8.5), what is the comparative efficacy of active coral outplanting (heat-evolved micro-fragments) versus Marine Protected Area (MPA) herbivory protection in averting macroalgal phase shifts between 2025 and 2050?*

### SRQ 5: Spatial Optimization for Restoration Prioritization
*How can multi-criteria spatial decision analysis (combining thermal refugia metrics, structural complexity, larval retention, and current degradation) identify the top 10% highest-impact reef zones for targeted ecological intervention?*
