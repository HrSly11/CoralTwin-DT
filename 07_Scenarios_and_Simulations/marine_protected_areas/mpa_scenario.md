# Scenario 4: Marine Protected Area (MPA) & Integrated Coral Conservation

## 1. Holistic Intervention Mechanics

- **No-Take Marine Reserve Enforcement:** Protection of parrotfish (*Scaridae*) and surgeonfish (*Acanthuridae*) boosting herbivory grazing capacity to $g = 0.68\text{ yr}^{-1}$.
- **Watershed Management:** Reduction of terrestrial nutrient and sediment runoff, maintaining water clarity ($K_d \le 0.06\text{ m}^{-1}$).
- **Targeted Larval Seeding:** Moderate assisted larval propagation in identified hydrodynamic source reefs ($\Phi_{\text{restoration}} = 1.8\% \text{ cover yr}^{-1}$).
