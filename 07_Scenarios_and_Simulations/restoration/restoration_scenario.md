# Scenario 3: Active Coral Restoration via Thermally Resilient Micro-Fragmentation

## 1. Intervention Strategy & Parameterization

- **Biological Technology:** Land-based ex-situ micro-fragmentation and selective breeding of thermally tolerant holobiont genotypes (e.g., *Acropora palmata*, *Porites astreoides* harboring *Durusdinium trenchii* symbionts).
- **Thermal Hardening Advantage:** Shifts individual bleaching threshold by $+2.0^\circ\text{C}$ (effectively buffering up to $8^\circ\text{C-weeks}$ DHW).
- **Outplanting Density:** Annual substrate seeding rate $\Phi_{\text{restoration}} = 2.8\% \text{ cover yr}^{-1}$.
- **Background Climatology:** Moderate-to-high warming pathway (mean DHW $7.0 - 12.0^\circ\text{C-weeks}$).
