# Scenario 1: Severe Thermal Stress (SSP5-8.5 Pathway)

## 1. Scenario Formulation & Climatological Forcing

- **Emissions Pathway:** Shared Socioeconomic Pathway SSP5-8.5 (Fossil-fueled development, $+4.4^\circ\text{C}$ warming by 2100).
- **Marine Heatwave (MHW) Frequency:** Mean recurrence interval $\tau_{\text{MHW}} = 1.4\text{ years}$.
- **Degree Heating Weeks (DHW):** Mean peak accumulation accelerating from $9.5^\circ\text{C-weeks}$ (2025) to $18.2^\circ\text{C-weeks}$ (2050).
- **Local Management:** Open-access fishing; baseline herbivory grazing capacity $g = 0.22\text{ yr}^{-1}$.

```
SST Forcing (2025-2050):
  2025: +1.1°C anomaly peak -> DHW ~ 9.5
  2035: +1.8°C anomaly peak -> DHW ~ 13.0
  2050: +2.6°C anomaly peak -> DHW ~ 18.2
```

## 2. Projected Ecosystem State (2050)

*Resultado obtenido mediante prototipo computacional del gemelo digital.*

- **Live Coral Cover:** Collapses from $32.0\%$ (2025) to **$4.8\%$ [2.1% – 8.3%]** by 2050.
- **Macroalgae Benthic Cover:** Expands from $22.0\%$ to **$71.4\%$**, inducing an irreversible phase-shift.
- **Net Calcification ($G_{\text{net}}$):** Becomes net negative ($-1.82\text{ kg CaCO}_3\text{ m}^{-2}\text{ yr}^{-1}$), causing structural reef flattening and loss of coastal protection.
