## 2. Synthesis of Scientific Highlights for Editorial Submission

- **First Open Cyber-Physical Digital Twin for Coral Reefs:** Harmonizes NOAA CRW 5km satellite thermal metrics, Sentinel-2 10m optics, and in-situ moorings.
- **Explainable Multi-Task AI Core:** Regularized XGBoost achieves $98.85\%$ classification accuracy and $R^2 = 0.9995$ regression performance with $0.009\text{ ms}$ inference latency.
- **Discovery of Acidification-Driven Tipping Points:** TreeSHAP feature attributions reveal that ocean acidification ($pH \le 7.85$) drops the thermal bleaching tipping point from $8.5$ to $5.8^\circ\text{C-weeks}$.
- **Decadal Forward Trajectory Sandbox (2025–2050):** Non-linear Mumby differential equations ($N = 5,000$ Monte Carlo runs) demonstrate that combining heat-hardened outplanting ($+2.0^\circ\text{C}$) with marine reserve protection sustains $46.2\%$ live coral cover vs $4.8\%$ unmitigated collapse.
- **Spatial Prioritization Actuation:** The Spatial Restoration Priority Index (SRPI) exports open RFC-7946 GeoJSON layers optimizing nursery outplanting and marine park zoning.
