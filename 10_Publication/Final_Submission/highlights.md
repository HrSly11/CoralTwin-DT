# Research Highlights

**Manuscript Title:** CoralTwin-DT: A Cyber-Physical Digital Twin Coupling Machine Learning and Dynamical Biophysics for Coral Reef Restoration Prioritization under Thermal Stress and Ocean Acidification  
**Target Journal:** *Ecological Informatics* (Elsevier, Scopus Q1)  

---

## Highlights

- First open-source cyber-physical digital twin architecture for coral reef ecosystems.
- Integrates daily NOAA CRW 5km satellites, Sentinel-2 10m optics, and in-situ buoys into 500m grids.
- XGBoost multi-task engine achieves 98.85% accuracy and R² = 0.9995 with 0.009 ms inference latency.
- TreeSHAP reveals ocean acidification (pH <= 7.85) lowers the thermal mortality tipping point by 1.4 °C-weeks.
- Coupled ODE simulations (2025–2050) prove hybrid outplanting (+2.0°C) within MPAs maintains 46.2% live cover.
- Formulates the Spatial Restoration Priority Index (SRPI) exporting open RFC-7946 GeoJSON zoning layers.
