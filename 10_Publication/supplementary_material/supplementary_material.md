# Supplementary Material: CoralTwin-DT

## Supplementary Information & Extended Data

**Manuscript Title:** Digital twin of coral reefs under thermal stress and ocean acidification for restoration and conservation prioritization  
**Authors:** CoralTwin-DT Doctoral Research Consortium  
**Target Journal:** *Global Change Biology* / *Ecological Informatics*  

---

### Table S1: Station Coordinates, Climatological Baselines & Geomorphic Classifications
Summary of all 25 benchmark monitoring stations across the Caribbean, Indo-Pacific, Red Sea, and Central Pacific provinces, including Maximum Monthly Mean (MMM) SST, baseline pH, and mean historical DHW exposure.

### Table S2: Machine Learning Hyperparameter Grid and Search Spaces
Detailed tuning grid for Random Forest, XGBoost, and Deep Multi-Layer Perceptron architectures.

### Table S3: Confusion Matrices by Geographic Province
Spatial breakdown of classification precision, recall, and false positive rates across Caribbean vs Indo-Pacific sub-basins.

### Table S4: Carbonate Chemistry Reaction Kinetics and Equilibrium Constants
Equilibrium constants ($K_1, K_2, K_{\text{sp}}$) used for stoichiometric dissolution of aragonite as functions of temperature and salinity.

### Table S5: Monte Carlo Parameter Priors and Sobol Sensitivity Indices
Prior probability density functions, bounds, and first-order ($S_i$) / total-order ($S_{Ti}$) Sobol indices for all dynamical ODE parameters.

---

### Supplementary Figure S1: Spatial Correlation Semi-Variograms
Semi-variograms of sea surface temperature anomalies and coral cover demonstrating spatial autocorrelation ranges and justifying the $25\text{ km}$ buffer in 5-Fold Spatially Stratified Cross-Validation.

### Supplementary Figure S2: XGBoost Feature Interaction Surfaces
Pairwise SHAP interaction values between Degree Heating Weeks ($DHW$) and Downwelling Solar PAR Irradiance ($PAR$).

### Supplementary Figure S3: Decadal Macroalgae Cover Trajectories (2025–2050)
Forward projections of fleshy macroalgal dominance across Scenarios 1–4 showing threshold tipping points.

### Supplementary Figure S4: Benthic Bathymetric Cross-Sections
Typical reef slope profile depicting spatial transition from Fore Reef to Reef Crest and Back Reef Lagoon.
