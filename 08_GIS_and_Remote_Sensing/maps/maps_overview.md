# GIS Maps & Spatial Distribution Overview

## 1. Regional Pilot Coverage

The digital twin GIS layer monitors key global coral hotspots:
- **Caribbean Province:** Mesoamerican Barrier Reef System (Mexico, Belize, Honduras), Florida Keys.
- **Indo-Pacific Province:** Coral Triangle (Raja Ampat, Komodo, Sulawesi), Great Barrier Reef (Northern & Central sectors), Okinawa (Kerama Islands).
- **Red Sea & Pacific:** Gulf of Aqaba (thermal refuge baseline), Hawaiian Archipelago, Palau.

---

## 2. Spatial Decision Layers

1. **Thermal Vulnerability Isoclines:** Spatial interpolation of peak historical DHW exposure.
2. **Benthic Structural Integrity:** Bathymetric slope and rugosity contours.
3. **Restoration Allocation Zones:** Hexagonal binning of Tier 1 intervention polygons.
